Guides

Practical notes for playing whereisshe.gg. Each one matches how the game actually works.

whereisshe.gg is a free daily geography game. When a date has a puzzle, you get five photos or short clips from one trip, you drop a pin for each, and the game scores the distance. These guides are the long version of the rules: the points formula, how to read a clip, how featured people opt in, and how the daily calendar differs from the archive. They are here so a player, and anyone reviewing the site, can learn the game without opening a round.

Nothing here is a walkthrough of today's photos. The guides do not name the places in a live puzzle, and they do not name the person until you have finished a day in the game itself. If a date has nothing scheduled, the archive still has earlier days, and these pages stay available either way.

  • How scoring and distance work. The 30 kilometre perfect score, the curve after that, and why a shared square is not the same thing as 5,000 points.
  • Reading location clues in a short clip. Signs, scripts, driving side, buildings, plants, light, and sound, in the order they are useful.
  • How featured people are chosen and credited. Opt-in, hand review, approval before a day is public, and a name that appears only after round five.
  • Daily puzzles and the archive. Local midnight, pinned dates, what "nothing scheduled" means, and why archive play does not move your streak.
  • A beginner geography clue guide. A fixed five-check order so the first pin is a region, then a country, then a town.
  • Guessing cities and the countryside. When to trust street text, and when to pin a region because the frame has no words.
  • Sharing a result and keeping a streak. Spoiler-free share text, the coloured squares, and which finishes count.
  • Using the map and reading the distance. Dropping a pin, the dashed line after Guess, and the summary of all five rounds.

The short version of a round is on How to play. Who runs the game, and the rule that pins are photo locations, is on About. To ask for a removal, a refund question, or anything else, use Contact.
