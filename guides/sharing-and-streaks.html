Sharing a result and keeping a streak

What your friends see, and what counts as playing today on whereisshe.gg.

Two different things you can send

After five rounds you can share a result or share the puzzle. They are not the same text. The result includes the day number, your total out of 25,000, one coloured square for each round, and a link back to that day. It does not include place names, distances in kilometres, or the featured person's name. Someone who has not played can open the link and still have a fair game. The puzzle share is the invitation: the day number, a line that says there are five travel photos, and the link. It has no score and no squares.

On a phone with a share sheet, the game uses that sheet. Otherwise it copies the text. You can send either one more than once. Sharing does not change the score. The squares use the same bands every day. Green is under 100 kilometres, yellow is under 500, orange is under 2,000, and red is anything farther. Those bands are wider than a perfect score. Perfect means within 30 kilometres and 5,000 points, which the scoring guide explains. A green square includes perfect guesses and also guesses that were merely close. A yellow square can still be a high score.

What a streak is

A streak is the number of daily puzzles you have finished on back-to-back local dates. Finish today's daily, and finish tomorrow's, and the streak becomes two. The date is your device's local calendar, the same date the daily uses. If you skip a day, the next daily you finish starts at one again. If your last finish was yesterday, today's streak is still alive before you play. If your last finish was earlier than yesterday, the streak is already at zero until you complete a new daily.

Only today's daily moves the streak. Archive games save a score for that older day and leave the streak alone. Opening a day and quitting before the fifth guess does not finish it. A preview used to check a day that is not public does not write a streak either. Your stats also keep games played, your average, your best total, your best streak, and a simple chart of daily totals. Those numbers come from dailies you finished, and they live in this browser.

Same phone, another phone, no account required

You can play with no account. The streak stays in the browser where you played. Clear my game data, in the footer, deletes that local copy. If you sign in, finished days can merge across devices, and the better total is kept when both devices scored the same day.

The results screen can also compare your total with other people who finished that same daily. Trust your own total and your squares first. They are computed from your pins. If you want the calendar rules behind "today" and the archive, they are in daily puzzles and the archive.

A spoiler-free habit

Send the result after you have looked at the traveler card, or send it before, as you prefer. The text itself will not name the person or the cities. If you talk about the round in the same message, you can spoil it with one place name. The link is enough. People who open it get the same five rounds you got, when that date has a puzzle pinned.
