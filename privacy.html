Privacy policy

Last updated Oct 23, 2026

This page explains what data WhereIsShe uses, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data.

If you just play (no account)

Your progress, scores, streak and settings (theme, sound) are stored only in your browser (localStorage). They aren't sent to us. “Clear my game data” in the menu deletes them.

If you sign in

  • Email sign-in or Google sign-in. We get your email address (and from Google, the basic profile Google shares with apps; we only use the email). It's used to sign you in, and to contact you about your submissions or Premium.
  • Stats sync. Your stats, saved results (your guesses and scores per puzzle) and settings are copied to your account so they're the same on every device. They're merged with the copy in your browser each time you sign in and after each game.
  • Accounts and synced data are stored with Supabase, our database and login provider. Each player can only read their own data.

Feature me submissions

  • We store what you send: your Instagram handle, your email, the links you paste, the photos and videos you upload, the location text you give for each, and your confirmations (that it's you, your consent, that you're 18+).
  • Uploads are kept in private storage (Supabase Storage), visible only to us for review. Nothing is public unless we feature you, and you approve your day first.
  • You need to be signed in (with a verified email) to submit. Each submission is linked to your account, so we can contact you and so that limits (one submission a day, a few waiting at a time) work. We don't store your IP address with submissions.

Premium payments

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). We never see or store your card details. Lemon Squeezy tells us that an order or subscription was paid, cancelled or refunded, with your account ID and email, so we can switch Premium on or off. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Ads and cookies

  • Free players may see ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure them, including personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage this at Google Ad Settings, and learn more in how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads or ad cookies are used.
  • Premium players don't load the ad script at all.
  • The game itself uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to save your progress and, if you sign in, your login session.

Other services the game loads

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap (and Esri as a fallback), and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details when your browser loads files from them.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Synced stats stay until you ask us to delete your account. Submissions are kept while we review them and, if featured, for as long as you're in the game. Ask us and we'll remove them.
  • To delete your account and synced data, or to remove your photos and profile link, email us from the address on your account (or with your Instagram handle). We'll confirm when it's done.
  • You can also ask for a copy of your data or to correct it.

Children

Submissions are only for people 18 or older. The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
