Contact

whereisshe.gg is run by one person. This is the address that reaches that person.

Email mikelyaps@gmail.com. The same address is on the About page, the privacy policy, the terms, and the refund page, and it is the address the game uses when a form falls back to email. Write from the email on your account if the message is about that account, a Feature me submission, or Premium. Include your Instagram handle if the message is about photos.

Removals

If you are featured, you can have your photos and profile link removed at any time, for any reason. You do not need to explain. Email from the address you used, or with the handle that appears on your traveler card. The footer of the game also has a removal link. Say that you want the photos and the profile link taken down, and you will get a confirmation when it is done.

Feature me and suggestions

To be in a future puzzle, use Feature me while signed in. You must be the person in the photos, be 18 or older, and agree to the use of the photos and the Instagram link. A day is reviewed by hand and you approve it before it is public. The home-page suggestion form is only for telling us about someone else. It is not consent, and it is not a submission of photos. If a form will not send, email the handle and a short note instead. Do not attach private documents, and do not send photos taken at or near your home.

Premium, refunds, and privacy

Premium is the optional subscription that removes ads on whereisshe.gg and on ranki.gg, and it moves your Feature me submission earlier in the review queue. Payments go through Lemon Squeezy. For a refund, follow the refund page: email within 14 days of the purchase and include the email you paid with. Questions about what the site stores, including AdSense cookies, are answered in the privacy policy. You can opt out of personalised Google ads at Google Ad Settings.

Bugs and the daily calendar

If a clip will not play, say the day number, the round, and the device. If a date says nothing is scheduled, that date has no pinned puzzle. The archive guide explains how to play earlier days. The game is family-friendly. Write if something on a published day does not belong in front of a general audience.

There is no separate support desk and no chat widget. Email is the whole contact path, and messages are read by hand.
